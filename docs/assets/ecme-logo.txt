Method: exact pixel-based background removal, approved by the owner after the built-in imagegen edit attempts altered fine details. The generated alternatives are not used in the website. The original JPEG is preserved without changes.

The preparation script removes the black matte and near-black JPEG noise, un-mattes antialiased edges, trims empty margins, and uses Lanczos resampling for delivery sizes. It does not trace, redraw, substitute lettering, or invent detail. A larger pixel size is not a claim of recovered source detail. The favicon crops the existing snowflake/sun ECME emblem from the supplied artwork.

Rebuild assets with: node scripts/prepare-brand-assets.mjs
